Nobody posts a photo of a porta potty.

Unless something went wrong with it. Honey Bucket units are at every big event from Washington to Texas, and they do their job quietly. That is the point of a great restroom. It also means the brand is invisible in the moments people actually share.

At events

Every guest becomes a brand promoter.

A balloon glow is the most photographed thing at any event. When the burners fire and the envelope lights up, phones come out. Put Honey Bucket on that envelope and everyone in the crowd is posting your brand to their friends, for free, because they want to.

The centerpiece

Your balloon anchors the glow line at festivals, fairs, concerts and game nights. It is the backdrop for every selfie.

Earned reach

Billboards get ignored. A glowing balloon gets photographed, tagged and shared by thousands of real people in your market.

Right where you work

The same events where your units are on site. Now the crowd sees Honey Bucket at its best, not just behind a door. Every glow photo is a ready-made #HoneyBucketSelfie.

Over the community

A flying Honey Bucket. People will post it.

Morning flights carry the balloon over neighborhoods across King and Pierce County, with Mount Rainier behind it. A giant Honey Bucket balloon drifting over the cul-de-sac at sunrise is the kind of thing people run outside to film. It is funny, it is friendly, and it gets shared.
